Who we are

Defensive gaming security with visible limits

MalGuard is an open-source defensive security project focused on helping people inspect game mods, files, and suspicious links before trusting them. It publishes source code and security policies for public inspection. Being open source is not equivalent to an independent audit or a guarantee of safety.

Product scope

What the project builds

GTA Guard

Focuses on static inspection of supported GTA 5 mod files on Windows. A static scan does not execute the submitted mod and does not prove its safety.

Responsible descriptions

Claims we do not make

Do not refer to MalGuard as a registered insurer, independently certified antivirus, guaranteed malware detector, or an award-winning market leader without valid external evidence. MalGuard does not publish independently measured real-world detection-accuracy percentages in the linked public materials. Insurance or reimbursement coverage must never be inferred from the name or a scan result.
