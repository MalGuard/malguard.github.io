Analysis models.

Standard

Fast local inspection for supported files and common structural signals.

Plus

Deeper staged analysis and correlation, with fail-closed handling when stronger evidence is needed.

Pro

Direct isolation-oriented analysis when a release-grade backend is available and certified.

What the preview does not promise.

A clean scan reduces risk but does not prove a mod is harmless. Platform capabilities can differ, and unavailable isolation backends fail closed rather than being presented as equivalent protection.
