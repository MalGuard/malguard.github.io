Open-source collaboration

Contribute to MalGuard.

We're welcoming contributors interested in AI security, backend APIs, full-stack development and defensive cybersecurity. This is a remote, voluntary and unpaid opportunity, not an employment offer.

How collaboration works

  1. Review our public projects and contribution guidelines.
  2. Introduce your skills, GitHub profile and weekly availability.
  3. If selected, agree on a scoped 7-day technical trial.
  4. After review, successful contributors may be invited to join the team.

Selection and team membership are not guaranteed. No paid work or competition prize is promised. Any future prize distribution requires separate agreement and must follow competition rules.

Apply to contribute

Send a short introduction including your GitHub profile, preferred area (AI security, backend, full-stack or research), and weekly availability. Please do not send credentials or sensitive information.
