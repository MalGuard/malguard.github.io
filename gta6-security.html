04 / VERIFY BEFORE YOU TRUST

A practical four-step checklist.

  1. 01
    Check the publisher independently.

    Visit the publisher's established website yourself. Do not rely on a logo or claim inside the suspicious page.

  2. 02
    Compare platform and availability claims.

    Offers for a supposedly available platform, demo, leak, or mod may contradict official announcements.

  3. 03
    Treat unknown installers and payment demands cautiously.

    Do not execute files just to find out what they do or supply credentials and wallet approvals to unverified sites.

  4. 04
    Keep 'unverified' separate from 'malicious'.

    An unknown reputation result is not proof of safety. An unusual URL alone does not prove malware.
